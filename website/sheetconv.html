Importing replaces everything in the app. Every account, transaction, category and person already in XPENC is swapped for this file's contents. XPENC saves a safety copy of your current data first, so you can undo it from Backup & Restore. Your theme and lock stay as they are.

Your XPENC file is ready

One file with everything above. It's made here in your browser.

Then, on your phone
  1. Copy the downloaded .json file to your phone (or download it there directly).
  2. Open XPENC → More → Backup & Restore.
  3. Tap Import from file, pick the file, and confirm.

Is my sheet uploaded?

No. It's read by a script running in this tab. Your progress is kept only in this browser — clear it any time from the footer.

What if my columns are unusual?

Every guess can be changed: pick the header row, re-map any column, and choose what each Type value or category column means.

Don't have XPENC yet?

It's free and works offline. Get it on Google Play or F-Droid, then import your file.
