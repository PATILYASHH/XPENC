Features

One rule everywhere: the numbers must not lie.

Net worth = the sum of your account balances. Transfers leave it unchanged, income raises it, expense lowers it. Everything else follows.

Share it in — SMS or screenshot

Share a bank SMS, or a Paytm/Google Pay/PhonePe payment screenshot, straight from your bank app — read entirely on-device (no READ_SMS permission, no network call), parsed and dropped into your Review Inbox to confirm.

Envelope Mode

Opt-in per account: every rupee gets a job. Assign money from Ready to Assign into a category before you can spend it — a real value container, not just a ceiling.

Budgets that speak up

Per-category budgets with period windows, live progress, and alerts at 80% and on overspend. A subcategory's cap can never exceed its parent's.

Dues & loans

Who owes what, partial settlements, running balances. Lending is not spending — dues stay out of your budgets.

Passcode lock

An optional 4, 5 or 6-digit PIN gates the app on open, with fingerprint or face unlock on top — your ledger stays yours even if your phone doesn't.

Goals & loans

Save toward a target with running progress and a contribution history, or track an EMI/loan's outstanding balance and log payments against it — plus named shopping lists and a home-screen net-worth widget.

Seven themes, real dark mode

True-black AMOLED dark, a violet "Colourful", a navy "Midnight", a near-black "Bold" with a coral & gold accent — and Cove, a One UI 9–inspired look with an ocean-blue accent and bigger, softer rounded cards.

Backup & export

Auto-backups land in your Downloads folder on a schedule, so they survive an uninstall. Plus PDF statements/reports and CSV export for accountants and Tally.

Also included

Honest accounts

Cash, Bank, Card & Prepaid balance — a payment is never counted twice.

Income · Expense · Transfer

Three types kept strictly apart. Transfers never pollute budgets or reports.

Calendar & reminders

Day-wise money-in/out grid, plus EMI and bill reminders that post nothing until you confirm.

Insights

Category breakdown down to subcategories, income vs expense and net-worth trend.

Import from CSV

Bank statement columns auto-detected, with a mapping screen to check before import.

Split payments

One purchase paid from more than one account posts as linked transactions.

Quick Add & widgets

Post an expense or income straight from a notification reply — no need to open the app.

Customize the bottom nav

Choose which two tabs sit next to the + button — Persons, Calendar, Budgets, Accounts, Stats or Payees — and optionally hide the text labels for an icon-only bar.

Text size & font

App-wide controls for text size, boldness and font family — bundled fonts included, no download needed.

Customize the dashboard

Flip any account out of the Net Worth total per-account — a goal you're tracking separately doesn't have to inflate it.

Master recovery phrase

Back up your PIN with a 10-word phrase, generated on-device and never stored in the clear — too many wrong PINs falls back to it instead of locking you out.

FAQ

Common questions.

The short answers. Everything here is also covered in more detail in the sections above or the privacy policy.

Is XPENC free to use?

Yes. XPENC is free and open source under the MIT license — no ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription, ever.

Does XPENC need an internet connection?

No. Release builds of the app don't request the INTERNET permission, so it's technically incapable of sending your data anywhere. (This website itself loads a font and a version badge online, but the Android app never does.)

Is my financial data safe and private?

Your accounts, transactions, budgets and dues live in a private SQLite database on your phone only. There's no server, no user accounts, no analytics and no trackers — and since the app is open source, every claim here can be verified by reading the code.

Can I import transactions from my bank?

Yes, three ways: share a bank SMS or a UPI payment screenshot (Paytm, Google Pay, PhonePe) into XPENC from the Share sheet, or import a CSV bank statement from Download Data. All three are read and parsed entirely on-device.

Does XPENC do budgeting and dues/loan tracking?

Yes — per-category budgets with live progress and overspend alerts, an opt-in Envelope Mode for zero-based budgeting, and separate dues & loans tracking for money you lend or borrow (kept out of your budgets).

Where can I download XPENC?

F-Droid (recommended — builds straight from source), a direct APK from GitHub Releases, or the OpenAPK mirror. A Google Play listing is coming soon.
