Take the tour

Everything your money needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Real screens from the app, with sample data. Tap any phone to see it full size.

Dashboard

Your whole money picture, on one screen.

Open the app and you already know where you stand — what you have, what came in, what went out, and what's due next.

  • Total money is the real sum of your balances — debit cards are never counted twice.
  • This month's income, expense and what you saved, at a glance.
  • People who owe you, and bills and salary coming up this week.
  • Pick a past month to see it exactly as it closed — and choose which widgets show, in your order.
Quick entry

Log a payment before the bill arrives.

A big numpad, your last account pre-selected, and colourful categories one tap away. Most entries take three taps.

  • Split one purchase across categories — or across the accounts that paid.
  • Tags, notes, receipt photos, a foreign-currency amount or your own emoji.
  • Or skip typing: share a bank SMS or a UPI screenshot into XPENC and it fills itself in, on-device.
Budgets

Budgets that speak up before it's too late.

Set a limit per category and watch it fill up as you spend. XPENC warns you at 80% and flags anything that goes over.

  • Live progress on every category, with a clear OVER badge when you cross the line.
  • Start your budget month on payday — any day from 1 to 28.
  • Optional Ready to Assign: give every rupee a job before you spend it.

People & groups

Who owes whom — settled in one tap.

Lend, borrow and split trips without a spreadsheet. Money you lend isn't an expense, so it never pollutes your budgets.

  • Split a group expense equally, by percentage or by exact amounts.
  • Partial repayments and a running balance for every person.
  • Pay or request straight through UPI, PayPal, Venmo, Cash App or Revolut.
  • Share a person's statement or a group's split as a PDF or an image card.
Goals & loans

Save toward what matters. See what loans really cost.

Goals show exactly how far you've come. Loans show the interest you'll pay, month by month — and what paying a little extra would save.

  • Savings goals with progress and a countdown to your target date.
  • Loans with interest rate, tenure, an EMI schedule and an interest/principal split.
  • A prepayment simulator and auto-paid EMIs, set up the moment you add a loan.
Stats & XPENC Score

Know if you're actually doing well.

The XPENC Score rates your money habits from 0 to 100 using seven rules — and tells you the one thing to improve next.

  • Nine modules: cash flow, spending, income, net worth, loans, people, goals, budgets and recurring.
  • Spending by category and subcategory, top transactions and top payees.
  • Share any month as a PDF report — generated on your phone.
Accounts & calendar

Every account, every date, accounted for.

Cash, bank, credit card and wallets side by side — and a calendar that shows exactly which days money moved.

  • Credit cards track their statement and due dates, with a reminder before it's due.
  • Accounts in any currency; net worth converts to your home currency.
  • Reminders for EMIs and bills post nothing until you confirm them.
CONECTR New
Backup & sync for XPENC

XPENC stays offline.
CONECTR carries the backup.

CONECTR is XPENC's separate online half. It saves the backup file XPENC already makes to a private repo in your own GitHub account, then brings it back on your other phone. It never opens XPENC's database — it only moves that file.

Your own private repo

Sign in with GitHub and your backups go to a private repo in your account — yours to open, download or delete.

Newest first, nothing lost

Each phone takes in the newest backup before it sends its own, so one phone never overwrites another's changes.

No file picking

Open XPENC and the backup CONECTR pulled comes in by itself. XPENC saves a copy of the phone's data first.

Friends by username

Add friends in CONECTR by username, then link them to your people in XPENC.

Set up in three steps

  1. Install CONECTRDownload the APK on your Android phone. Android may ask you to allow installing apps from your browser.
  2. Sign in with GitHubCONECTR keeps XPENC's backups in a private repo of yours, XPENC-BACKUP.
  3. Open XPENC on your other phoneThe newest backup comes in by itself — on a computer, XPENC web joins in from Settings → Connector.

Good to know

  • XPENC itself still has no internet access. Only CONECTR goes online, and only if you install it.
  • Your backup sits in your repo as the same file XPENC writes — private to you, but not encrypted. Sign-in runs through Supabase.
  • Automatic import needs XPENC 1.6.6 or later (GitHub or F-Droid build). With older XPENC, use Import from file.
  • XPENC from the Play Store isn't recognised yet.
  • CONECTR is free and Android-only for now.

Features

One rule everywhere: the numbers must not lie.

Net worth is the sum of your account balances. Transfers leave it unchanged, income raises it, expense lowers it. Everything else follows from that.

XPENC on your computer New

Open xpenc.in/web — nothing to install. Laid out for a PC: a sidebar, a two-column dashboard, transactions as a table, amounts typed on the keyboard. Works offline; data stays in that browser.

Modes for trips & events New

Switch a trip on and the app is the trip: what's left to spend and per day, limits that ask before you go over, the people with you and a trip wallet.

Your colours, your currencies New

Choose the colours for money in, money out, transfers and people, add currencies of your own, and give each account its own icon and colour.

Share as a PDF or an image

A transaction as a receipt, a person's statement with a running balance, a group's who-owes-whom — as a clean PDF or a styled card, made on your phone.

Hide from screenshots

Mark a transaction private and it shows as frosted glass everywhere it's listed, so a screenshot catches only the frost. Hold it to peek.

Your dashboard, your way

Drag widgets into order, add Cash flow, Goals, Loans, Groups or Shopping lists, and pick any past month to see it exactly as it closed.

SMS & screenshot capture

On Android, share a bank SMS or a Paytm, Google Pay or PhonePe screenshot into XPENC. It's parsed on-device into your Review Inbox — no READ_SMS permission.

Ready to Assign

Zero-based budgeting: every account pools into one figure, and you fund each category before you spend — funded, underfunded or overspent at a glance.

Multi-currency

Accounts in any currency, statements in that currency, net worth in yours. Pick your country on first run and amounts look right from day one.

Auto rules

Daily to yearly schedules, promo prices for the first few months, "Pay now", and rules that fund a goal or pay an EMI by themselves.

App lock

PIN, master password and/or an authenticator app, fingerprint or face unlock, Scrambled numpads and a 10-word recovery phrase.

Backups that survive

On Android, automatic backups to Downloads that outlive an uninstall. On iPhone, share one to Files or iCloud Drive. Either restores on the other.

Import from CSV or Excel

Bank statement CSVs are auto-detected in the app. Kept your money in Excel? SheetConv converts it in your browser.

Credit card due dates

Set the statement close day and payment due day to see the current billing cycle, days left and a reminder before it's due.

Calculators

FD & RD, loan EMI, SIP & lumpsum, PPF, GST and income tax for India, the US, the UK and Germany — right inside the app (Beta).

Templates & your own emoji

Reusable transaction templates, duplicate with a long-press, saved category sets — and 76 searchable icons or any emoji you like as a category's icon.

Quick Add & widgets

On Android, post an expense straight from a notification, add a This Month widget, and lock-screen shortcuts for the things you do most.

Make it yours

Glass, Classic or Noir in light, dark or system mode. Pick your bottom-nav tabs, hide amounts on demand, and set text size, boldness and font.

FAQ

Common questions.

The short answers. The privacy policy has the long ones.

Is XPENC free to use?

Yes. XPENC is free and open source under the MIT license — no ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription, ever.

Does XPENC need an internet connection?

No. Release builds for Android don't request the INTERNET permission, so the app is technically incapable of sending your data anywhere. On iPhone, where apps can't give that permission up, XPENC still never makes a network request. (This website loads a font and a version badge online; the app never does.)

Is my financial data safe and private?

Your accounts, transactions, budgets and dues live in a private SQLite database on your phone only. There's no server, no user accounts, no analytics and no trackers — and since the app is open source, every claim here can be verified by reading the code.

Can I import transactions from my bank?

Yes, three ways: share a bank SMS or a UPI payment screenshot (Paytm, Google Pay, PhonePe) into XPENC from the Share sheet, or import a CSV bank statement from Download Data. All three are read and parsed entirely on-device.

Can I move my Excel or Google Sheets tracker into XPENC?

Yes. SheetConv converts an Excel, Google Sheets or CSV money tracker into a file XPENC imports from More › Backup & Restore › Import from file. It runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Does XPENC do budgeting and dues/loan tracking?

Yes — per-category budgets with live progress and overspend alerts, an optional Ready to Assign layer for zero-based budgeting, loans with interest, EMI schedules and a prepayment simulator, and separate dues tracking for money you lend or borrow, kept out of your budgets.

Where can I download XPENC?

On Android, Google Play is the easiest — it installs and updates automatically. F-Droid builds straight from source, and there's also a direct APK from GitHub Releases. On iPhone, use SideStore or AltStore, or the .ipa from GitHub Releases. All of them are free and built from the same code.

Is XPENC available on iPhone?

Yes. XPENC for iPhone (iOS 15 or later) installs with SideStore or AltStore: add the source xpenc.in/ios/source.json and new versions arrive there. Or sideload the .ipa from GitHub Releases with your own Apple ID. It isn't on the App Store. A backup made on Android restores on iPhone, and the other way round. How to install.

Can I use XPENC on my computer?

Yes. Open xpenc.in/web in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari — nothing to install. It lays out for a big screen and works offline after the first visit. Your data stays in that browser; bring a phone's backup over with More › Backup & Restore › Import from file. Things that need a phone — sharing SMS or screenshots in, fingerprint unlock, widgets and notifications — aren't there.

What is CONECTR?

XPENC's optional backup & sync companion for Android, from ExoSynk Labs. It saves the backup file XPENC already makes to a private repo in your own GitHub account, brings it back on your other phone, and lets you link friends to your people. XPENC itself still has no internet access — only CONECTR goes online, and only if you install it. More about CONECTR.

Who makes XPENC?

Yash Patil, as part of ExoSynk Labs — the ExoSynk team's small, offline-first apps, made alongside the ExoSynk circuit lab. XPENC stays free and open source under the MIT license.
