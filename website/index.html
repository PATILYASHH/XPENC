Features

One rule everywhere: the numbers must not lie.

Net worth = the sum of your account balances. Transfers leave it unchanged, income raises it, expense lowers it. Everything else follows.

Honest accounts

Cash, Bank, Card & Prepaid balance (a canteen fob or gift card) with real balances. UPI and debit cards spend their bank's money — a payment is never counted twice.

Income · Expense · Transfer

Three transaction types kept strictly apart. Tag anything and attach a receipt photo. Transfers never pollute budgets or reports.

Budgets that speak up

Per-category budgets with period windows, live progress, and alerts at 80% and on overspend. A subcategory's cap can never exceed its parent's.

Dues & loans

Who owes what, partial settlements, running balances. Lending is not spending — dues stay out of your budgets.

Calendar & reminders

Day-wise money-in / money-out grid. EMI and bill reminders that post nothing until you confirm.

Insights

Category breakdown, income vs expense, net-worth trend and per-account reports.

Backup & export

Auto-backups land in your Downloads folder on a schedule, so they survive an uninstall. Plus PDF statements/reports and CSV export for accountants and Tally.

Bank-SMS capturesoon

Paused in 1.1 for Play compliance. The on-device pipeline — parser, dedupe, review cards, undo — is ready and waiting.

Passcode lock

An optional PIN gates the app on open, with fingerprint or face unlock on top — your ledger stays yours even if your phone doesn't.

Savings goals

Save toward a target with running progress and a contribution history — plus named shopping lists and a home-screen net-worth widget.

Envelope Mode

Opt-in per account: every rupee gets a job. Assign money from Ready to Assign into a category before you can spend it — a real value container, not just a ceiling.

Screens

What it actually looks like.

Dashboard, transactions, budgets, accounts and calendar — the whole app, running entirely offline on your phone.

Privacy

The app can't leak your data. It has no internet.

Release builds don't request the INTERNET permission, so transmitting your data is technically impossible — not a promise, a property. Verify it yourself: the code is open.

No INTERNET permission No SMS permission No analytics No account No cloud

Before you report a bug

Include your Android version, XPENC version (Settings → About) and the exact steps. A screenshot helps more than a paragraph.

Your data stays yours

Never send me your backup file unless I ask — and even then, it's your call. I can debug from steps alone.

Response time

One maintainer, so usually a day or two. GitHub gets checked most often.

Read every line.
