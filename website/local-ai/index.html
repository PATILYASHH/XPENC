How it works

Turn on, download, import, ask Exo.

Needs XPENC 1.6.7 or newer on Android — in the beta for now. On iPhone, Local AI comes later. XPENC web doesn't have it: a browser can't run a model like a phone can.

  1. Turn onIn XPENC, go to Settings › Local AI and switch on Use Local AI.
  2. DownloadTap Download next to a model above. It lands in your phone's Downloads.
  3. ImportBack in Settings › Local AI, tap Import and pick the file. XPENC checks it runs on your phone.
  4. Ask ExoTap ✨ on the Dashboard, or Explain my month in Stats. With a voice model, tap 🎤 and speak.
lock Nothing leaves your phone

XPENC has no internet permission. The model runs on your phone's own processor, and your questions, answers and voice recordings are never sent anywhere.

visibility Only reads, never writes

The AI can't add, edit or delete anything. XPENC works out the figures itself and shows them under every answer, so you can check what it said.

memory AI Memory is yours

Tell it what matters — "my salary comes on the 1st". Only you add or remove these, in Settings › Local AI › AI Memory. They're in your backups.

Good to know: AI can be wrong, especially the Small model. It doesn't give investment, tax or legal advice. A model needs as much free space as its size, and the first answer takes a few seconds while it loads. Remove a model any time from Settings › Local AI.
