How to use one

Download, import, switch.

Needs XPENC 1.6.6 or newer (now in beta). Nothing changes in the app until you switch to the template.

  1. DownloadPick a template above and tap Download. You get one small .json file.
  2. ImportIn XPENC, open More › Categories, tap Templates in the top bar, then Import from file.
  3. SwitchThe template is saved next to your own. Switch to it whenever you're ready, and switch back any time.

Your history stays put. When you switch, categories with the same name (Rent, Food, Groceries and so on) move into place along with their transactions. Past transactions always keep the category they already have.

Made a set that works for you? Share it.

Use Share as file on any saved template in XPENC and open a pull request on GitHub. Once it's merged, it shows up here for everyone. The file never contains your amounts or transactions.
