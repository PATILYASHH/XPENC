How it works

Download, open, done.

Needs XPENC 1.6.6 or newer. To get here from the app, go to Settings › General › Language › More languages.

  1. DownloadTap Download next to your language. You get one small .json file.
  2. Open with XPENCOn Android, tap Open when the download finishes, or open the file from Files › Downloads, and choose XPENC.
  3. DoneXPENC switches to that language straight away. To change it again, go to Settings › General › Language.

On iPhone, or if XPENC isn't offered: in XPENC, go to Settings › General › Language, tap Import language pack and pick the file you downloaded. A pack doesn't touch your accounts or transactions. Exported PDFs and spreadsheets stay in English.

Spot a wrong word? Help finish a language.

Each pack is a plain file that maps XPENC's English text to your language. Where a pack has no translation yet, that text stays in English. Corrections and new translations are welcome on GitHub, and they reach this page with the next update.
